## Subnet Idea: Vigil

**Status:** Final Proposal Ready

**One-liner:** Miners compete to predict DeFi liquidations; validators verify against on-chain events.

**The pitch:**
- $2.4B liquidated in DeFi last year
- Borrowers had no warning
- Liquidators with private bots won everything
- Vigil decentralizes this intelligence

**Why it works:**
- Novel use case (no Bittensor subnet does this)
- Objectively verifiable (liquidations are on-chain facts)
- Buildable in 4 weeks (focused scope)
- Protocol-agnostic architecture (Aave + Compound + Morpho = 65% market)
