## Thread

### 1/5 - The Problem
```
A whale deposits $10M into Aave.

Market drops 15%. Health factor hits 1.02.

Two people are about to lose:

1. The borrower - no warning, loses 10% to penalties
2. The slow liquidator - someone else gets there first

Prediction intelligence exists. It's just locked in private bots.
```

### 2/5 - The Solution
```
Sentinel changes this:

Miners compete to predict which positions will liquidate - and when.

Validators verify predictions against actual on-chain events.

Accurate predictions = TAO rewards
False alarms = penalties

Simple. Objective. Decentralized.
```

### 3/5 - Why It Works
```
Liquidations are on-chain facts.

No subjectivity. No "quality" judgments.

Either a position was liquidated or it wasn't.
Either it happened when you predicted or it didn't.

Transaction hash = proof.
Block timestamp = timing.

Perfect fit for Bittensor's verification model.
```

### 4/5 - Who Benefits
```
Liquidators: Better timing = more profit

Borrowers: Early warning = time to add collateral

Protocols: Risk visibility = better UX

Everyone wins except the information hoarders.
```
