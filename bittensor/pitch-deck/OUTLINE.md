# Pitch Deck Outline (10 Pages)

## Page 1: Title
- Subnet Name
- One-line tagline
- Team name/logo

## Page 2: The Problem
- Pain point (make it visceral)
- Market size / scope of problem
- Who suffers from this

## Page 3: The Solution
- What the subnet does (simple explanation)
- Key insight / unique approach
- Visual diagram

## Page 4: How It Works
- High-level architecture
- Miner → Validator → Reward flow
- Simple visual

## Page 5: Mechanism Design
- Incentive structure
- Why miners participate
- Why validators stay honest
- Anti-gaming measures

## Page 6: Why Bittensor?
- Why decentralized > centralized for this use case
- Why Bittensor specifically
- Network effects

## Page 7: Market Opportunity
- TAM/SAM/SOM (if applicable)
- Growth trajectory
- Why now

## Page 8: Competitive Landscape
- 2x2 matrix or comparison table
- Our positioning
- Defensibility

## Page 9: Go-To-Market
- Target users
- Distribution strategy
- Bootstrapping plan

## Page 10: Ask / Vision
- What we're building toward
- What we need (if seeking investment)
- Call to action

---

## Design Notes

- Clean, minimal design
- One key point per slide
- Visuals > walls of text
- Use Bittensor brand colors where appropriate
- Export as PDF
