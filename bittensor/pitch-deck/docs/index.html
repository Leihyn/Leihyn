Vigil Overview

Decentralized liquidation prediction on Bittensor

The Problem

A user deposits $100,000 into Aave as collateral and borrows $70,000. Market drops 20% overnight. Their health factor crosses 1.0. They get liquidated.

No warning. No alert. Just a 10% penalty - $7,000 gone.

Meanwhile, a liquidator with a private prediction bot saw this coming 3 hours ago. They positioned themselves, executed the liquidation, and pocketed the bonus.

$2.4 billion was liquidated across DeFi in 2024. The borrowers who lost had no warning. The liquidators who won had proprietary prediction systems.

This intelligence exists. It's just locked inside private bots.

The Solution

Vigil is a Bittensor subnet where miners compete to predict DeFi liquidations before they happen.

  • Miners analyze on-chain positions and predict which will be liquidated
  • Validators verify predictions against actual liquidation events
  • Accurate predictions earn TAO (Bittensor's native token)

The result: Decentralized liquidation intelligence that anyone can access.

How It Works

EVERY EPOCH (1 hour):

1. Validators snapshot at-risk positions (1.0 < HF < 1.5)
2. Miners receive position list
3. Miners have 10 minutes to analyze and submit predictions
4. Observation window runs for 6 hours
5. Validators match predictions to actual liquidations
6. TAO distributed to top performers

Key Innovations

Danger Zone Partial Credit

If a miner predicts liquidation but the borrower adds collateral and saves the position, they still get partial credit if the position entered the "danger zone" (HF < 1.02). This provides fairer scoring and more data points.

Rolling 24-Hour Aggregation

With only 5-10 liquidations per day, scoring per epoch is statistically noisy. Vigil aggregates scores over a rolling 24-hour window for statistical significance.

Protocol-Agnostic Architecture

Vigil supports multiple lending protocols through a unified adapter interface. Launch includes Aave V3, Compound V3, and Morpho - covering ~65% of the DeFi lending market.

Historical Replay Mode

Testnet has no liquidations? Use mainnet data from 30 days ago to prove the mechanism works before going live.
