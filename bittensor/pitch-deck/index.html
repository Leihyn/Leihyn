The Problem

Borrowers have no warning.

When a DeFi position gets liquidated, the borrower loses 5-15% in penalties. Meanwhile, liquidators with private prediction bots saw it coming hours ago. The intelligence exists - it's just locked in private systems.

The Solution

How Vigil Works

Miners compete to predict liquidations. Validators verify against on-chain facts. Best predictors earn TAO.

1

Miners Predict

Analyze at-risk positions and predict which will be liquidated in the next 6 hours

2

Validators Verify

Check predictions against actual on-chain liquidation events - no subjectivity

3

TAO Rewards

Accurate predictors earn TAO from network emissions (like Bitcoin mining)

Key Innovations

What Makes Vigil Different

Danger Zone Partial Credit

If a position hit critical risk levels but was rescued, miners get half credit. Fairer scoring with more data points.

Protocol-Agnostic Architecture

Supports Aave V3, Compound V3, and Morpho out of the box. Add new protocols by implementing one interface.

Rolling 24-Hour Aggregation

With only 5-10 liquidations per day, per-epoch scoring is too noisy. 24-hour windows provide statistical significance.

Historical Replay Mode

Testnet has no liquidations? Use mainnet data from 30 days ago to prove the mechanism works.
