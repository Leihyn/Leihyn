About the Series

DeFi shouldn't be confusing. It just needs the right story.

DeFi Unlocked uses visual storytelling to break down complex decentralized finance concepts — liquidity pools, veTokens, protocol comparisons, and more — into something anyone can understand.

Follow Kael, a blockchain-native guide, and Nia, a curious newcomer, as they explore the world of DeFi together.
