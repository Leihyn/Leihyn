## Why This Role

The L2 landscape has an explanation problem. The content that exists is either marketing (too shallow) or research papers (too dense). The people making decisions - developers choosing stacks, institutions evaluating risk, builders allocating attention - need something in between.

I've spent the last year proving that visual storytelling works for complex crypto concepts. Zero-knowledge proofs are arguably harder to explain than rollup architecture, and I turned them into a hackathon-winning comic series. I want to do the same for Ethereum's L2 ecosystem - make the security guarantees, tradeoffs, and nuances something people actually understand instead of something they pretend to.
